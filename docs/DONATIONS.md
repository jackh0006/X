# Donations

All addresses below are public receiving addresses published by **jackh0006** in
the canonical `X-Wallet/DONATE.md` (itself sourced from the public
`BNB-Terminal-Wallet` repository) and in the `X-Messenger` README/FUNDING file.
They were reproduced exactly, then checked with
`scripts/validate_donations.py`.

Validation covers address format and checksum only. It does **not** prove
ownership or that an address is still monitored. Verify the full address in at
least two official locations before sending. Crypto transfers are irreversible.
No private key, seed phrase, or recovery phrase is published anywhere in this
repository.
